# Tier P1b — Prior TBCP awardees moving into operations — DRAFT

**Who:** network, IT or enterprise-operations lead at a tribal organization
that already received a Tribal Broadband Connectivity Program award in an
earlier round, where the build is moving toward operations.
**Not for:** current-round (TBCP3) or NEGP applicants, grant-writing asks, or
anyone whose award cannot be confirmed on NTIA's public award listing.
**Use only if no warm path exists** (see `warm-intros.md`).
**CTA (only one):** request a One-Site Snapshot — `https://konative.com/contact#request`

Hard rules for this tier:

- **Konative does not write grants and is not a TBCP3/NEGP applicant.** No
  copy offers grant help, application help, or deadline reminders. No deadline
  dates of any kind.
- The award fact (claim C7) must be confirmed on NTIA's public award page and
  the URL pasted into the Twenty record before approval. **No award amount in
  the email.** The July 2026 campaign had to correct a TBCP funding claim; do
  not repeat that.
- Do not open with congratulations on the award (messaging lock). Open with
  the operations question.

Spacing: 5–7 business days between touches. Touches 2–3 are replies in the
Touch 1 thread. Stop on any reply, bounce or opt-out.
