## This issue: three checks before your next renewal

Most network outages that hurt a casino, clinic or government office are not
exotic. They come from a few ordinary gaps. Here are three checks any IT lead
can run with the contracts and invoices already on file.

**1. Are your two carriers really two paths?**
Two providers on the invoice can still ride the same fiber, conduit or building
entrance. Ask each carrier, in writing, for the route and the entrance their
circuit uses into your building. If the answers overlap, you have one path with
two bills.

**2. Do your contracts renew on one calendar?**
Put every circuit on one list: carrier, service, monthly cost, term end, and
auto-renew notice period. Contracts that renew at scattered times tend to roll
over without anyone shopping them. One calendar is what lets you make carriers
compete.

**3. Does the failover actually fail over?**
A backup circuit nobody has tested is a guess. Schedule a planned failover test
in a maintenance window and write down what stayed up (payments, surveillance,
voice, guest Wi-Fi) and what didn't.
