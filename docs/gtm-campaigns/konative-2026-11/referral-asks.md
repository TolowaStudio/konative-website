# Referral asks to existing contacts — DRAFT

**Warm only.** 1:1 messages Jeramey sends himself to people he already knows:
past and current clients, partners, tribal peers, industry friends,
first-degree LinkedIn connections he has actually talked with. No campaign
tool. Same eligibility and do-not-contact rules as `follow-ups.md`.

Differs from `warm-intros.md`: warm intros ask a partner about **one named
account**. A referral ask asks a friend **who they know**, with no target
named.

Make each ask easy: one question, a forwardable blurb, and an easy no.

---

## 1. General referral ask (email or LinkedIn message)

**Subject:** A small favor

Hi [First name],

Hope you're doing well. A quick favor, and "no one comes to mind" is a fine
answer.

Konative helps tribal enterprises and data-center projects source internet,
fiber, SD-WAN and voice. We compare suppliers, check that backup circuits
really run on separate paths, and stay involved through installation and
renewal. Suppliers pay our fee; the customer doesn't.

Do you know anyone who has a contract renewal, a new site, or a network
that's had one outage too many? If so, would you be comfortable forwarding
the note below?

Thanks either way.

Jeramey

> **Forwardable:** Jeramey James runs Konative, a Native-owned, vendor-neutral
> connectivity brokerage. He'll do a free one-page snapshot for one site:
> which carriers reach it, whether backup paths are really separate, and when
> contracts renew. Suppliers pay his fee, not you.
> https://konative.com/contact#request

> happened next.] I owe you one.

Send within a day of the intro, and again once the referral turns into a

---

## Rules

- At most one referral ask per person per quarter.
- Never send a referral ask to someone on the do-not-contact list, or to
  anyone Jeramey doesn't actually know.
- No referral fees or gifts are offered in these drafts. Any referral payment
  is a money decision for Jeramey and would need checking against the Avant
  agreement first.
- Log every ask and every referral in Twenty (source = referral + who).
