# Follow-ups to existing relationships — DRAFT

**Warm only (Jeramey, 2026-11-15, TOL-3559).** These are plain 1:1 messages
Jeramey writes and sends himself from his own mailbox or LinkedIn. No campaign
tool, no sequencer, no merge-and-blast.

## Who is eligible

Only people with a **two-way history** recorded in Twenty:

- someone Jeramey knows personally, or met and spoke with;
- someone who was introduced through a warm intro (`warm-intros.md`);
- someone who replied to Konative, filled in the konative.com form, booked a
  call, or opted in to the newsletter.

**Not eligible:** anyone whose only history is a one-way cold message, which
includes the ~101 recipients of the July 2026 bulk send who never replied. A
cold send we made doesn't create a relationship. Also not eligible: anyone on
the do-not-contact list (`COMPLIANCE.md` §3).

## How to use

Pick the paragraph that fits what the person cares about, then rewrite it in
your own words for that person. **One message, then at most one follow-up**
5–7 business days later if there's no reply. After that, stop. Stop at once on
"no", "not now" or any request not to be contacted.

The one ask in every message: a free One-Site Snapshot,
`https://konative.com/contact#request` (or "just reply and I'll set it up").
