that is under construction (public signal required, claim C8).
**Not for:** hyperscaler IRU buyers, projects with no public construction
signal.
**Use only if no warm path exists** (see `warm-intros.md`).
**CTA (only one):** request a One-Site Snapshot — `https://konative.com/contact#request`

Spacing: Touch 1 → Touch 2 after 5–7 business days → Touch 3 after another
5–7. Touches 2 and 3 are sent as replies in the Touch 1 thread; "Re:" is used
